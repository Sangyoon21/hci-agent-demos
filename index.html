Agentic AI demos

Two interactive prototypes for a university HCI critique. Each runs entirely in your browser with an offline mock AI; all data is synthetic and nothing is sent anywhere.

Claim Rescue →

Turns insurance claim denials into cited appeal drafts that a doctor approves. Deterministic guards catch paraphrased quotes, uncited claims and evidence from the wrong patient.

 CostGuard →

Finds idle cloud machines, pauses test machines on its own, asks a person before touching production, and blocks a prompt-injection attempt.

 Simulated: EHR/insurer systems, cloud provider, Slack and billing data.
